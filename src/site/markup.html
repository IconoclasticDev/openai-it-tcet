Operating Manifesto

This isn't a club that collects registrations. It's a working committee that ships technical output.

Build-first membership. Every member writes code, reproduces papers, or builds real tools. No passive participation.

Open research culture. We read, discuss, and re-implement frontier AI papers — not just summarize them on slides.

Department-backed credibility. Open AI IT operates under the Department of Information Technology, TCET — with direct faculty oversight and institutional support.
